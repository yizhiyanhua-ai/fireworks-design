Manifesto

II design interfaces the way a typesetter sets a page — every element earns its millimetre. Restraint is not a style; it is a decision made a thousand times an hour. I am suspicious of delight that asks nothing of the user, and I would rather a product be quietly legible than loudly impressive.

Product Designer — Interfaces, Systems, Soft Realism.
