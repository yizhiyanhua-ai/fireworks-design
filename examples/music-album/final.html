— Liner notes

The sound of 3 a.m.,
held in violet light.

AURORA is the sound of 3 a.m. — violet light on skin, the static between two heartbeats, the slow bloom of a feeling you can't name until the chorus hits. NOVA trades the dance floor for the after-party, the after-party for the drive home, the drive home for the long moment before sleep. Press play and the room goes indigo.

"I wanted to make an album that feels like the inside of a slow exhale."

— NOVA, on AURORA
