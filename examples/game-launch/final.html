// INTERCEPTED · FRAGMENT 01

In the silence between the stars, something is answering.

The Verity-Annan went dark forty-three days ago, somewhere past the rim of the charted void. You are the salvage contractor they sent after it. You are alone, you are under-supplied, and you are the first human being to hear what its hull has been repeating — in a frequency that should not carry voice.

ECHOES OF THE VOID is a cinematic survival epic set aboard derelicts that drift through unmapped sectors. Each wreck is a self-contained mystery, each one a place where the laws of physics learned to whisper. Salvage what you can. Repair what fails. Decide, every hour, what you are willing to become to keep breathing.

There is no cavalry. There is no map home. There is only the cold instrument-light of your suit, the slow decay of your oxygen, and the growing certainty that the thing answering you has been waiting a very long time to be understood.

Single-player · 2-player co-op · ~40hr campaign
§ 02 — Core Systems

Built to survive.
Engineered to unnerve.

Four systems define every hour aboard the wreck. None of them are kind. All of them are listening.

SYS_01

Zero-G Salvage Combat

Tether, breach, and strip derelicts in full six-axis zero-gravity. Every hull plate you pry loose is one more minute of oxygen — and one more thing the wreck has noticed about you.

SYS_02

Living, Dying Starmap

The sector is not static. Stars collapse, lanes shift, and yesterday's safe route is tonight's ambush. Chart it yourself — or trust the corrupted logs of the dead.

SYS_03

Adaptive Soundsystem

A reactive score that learns your fear. Silence is the default state; music arrives only when the ship decides you have earned its attention — and it always decides too late.

SYS_04

Solo or Co-op Void

Endure alone, or bring one trusted operator into drop-in co-op. Shared oxygen, shared dread, shared blame. There is no respawning in the cold — only what you chose to leave behind.
