01 — Our Work

Water is the beginning of everything that follows.

Right now, 771 million people wake up every day without clean water close to home. The cost isn’t only health — it’s hours, education, income, and agency. Women and girls alone spend an estimated 200 million hours every day walking for water.

But the people we serve are not a problem to be solved. They are partners with plans, families with dreams, communities ready to lead. What they lack is infrastructure — and that, unlike pity, is something we can build.

Our work is simple: lasting, locally-led, GPS-verified water access — then a handover, not a handout.

“Water doesn’t end poverty. It makes ending poverty possible.”
