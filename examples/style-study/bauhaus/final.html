§ 01 — Curriculum

Four disciplines. One grammar.

Each course isolates a single element of the modernist vocabulary. Mastery is demonstrated by restraint — the ability to remove everything that is not load-bearing.

§ 03 — Manifesto

Three principles, set in order.

  1. 01

    Form follows function. The page must do its work before it asks to be admired.

  2. 02

    Three primaries are enough. Discipline is not a limitation; it is the medium.

  3. 03

    The grid is a moral position. Everything in its place — or remove it.
