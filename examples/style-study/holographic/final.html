The lab

Color is a frequency.

PRISM is not a makeup brand. It is a color lab where pigment is engineered at the wavelength level — every shade a measured band of refracted light, suspended in a texture you can wear.

We do not chase glow. We tune frequency. Each foil, gloss, and chrome flake is calibrated against the spectrum, so the same cyan that reads cool on your wrist reads electric on your lid.

“We don’t make color. We make light, hold still.”
