Our story

A workshop,
not a factory.

Every HYGGE & CO. piece is made in small batches and finished by hand. Nothing leaves the bench identical to the last — a glaze pools differently, a weave settles where the wool decides. We consider that the point, not the flaw.

The wool comes from Faroe sheep grazing open pasture. The flax is retted in Flemish fields. The clay is dug from Swedish earth outside Höganäs. The wax is poured from local honeycomb in Småland. Four materials, four places, all named.

Warmth isn't a finish you apply. It's what's left when you remove everything that isn't necessary.

— Mette Sørensen, founder
