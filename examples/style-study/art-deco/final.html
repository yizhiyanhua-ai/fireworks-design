The Room

Brass banquettes, a single chandelier dimmed low, and walls the colour of a closed cigar box. The bar is the brightest thing in the room, and only just.

The Ritual

The door. The wait. The hush when it opens. We do not hurry the threshold — the threshold is part of the hour you have come to keep.
