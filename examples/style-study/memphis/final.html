How to start a riot

Learn it in 30 seconds. Lose friends over it for years.

1

DRAW

The judge pulls a prompt card and reads it aloud. Something unhinged. Something that demands a terrible answer.

2

PLAY

Everyone slams down a hidden answer — face-down, anonymous, zero accountability. The judge shuffles and reads them all out loud.

3

RIOT

The judge picks the funniest answer. The winner takes the round card as a point. The table riots. Repeat until someone knocks something over.

Try it. We dare you.
