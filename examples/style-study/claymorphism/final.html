How it works

Three soft steps to a daily bloom

No setup marathon. No lesson planning. You tell us about your kid — we handle the rest, gently.

  1. 1

    Pick your kid’s age & interests

    A 60-second setup. Slide to their age, tap a few things they love — dinosaurs, drawing, space, fairy tales. That’s it.

  2. 2

    BLOOM builds a daily 10-min path

    Each morning, a fresh mini-quest across Math, Reading, Science, and Art — paced to your child, never too hard, never too easy.

  3. 3

    Kid plays, parent gets a weekly bloom report

    Every Sunday: a warm, jargon-free summary of what they explored, where they grew, and a single gentle next step. No dashboards to babysit.
